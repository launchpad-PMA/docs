# Advocacy Launchpad PMA Membership Covenant

## Temple of Roots Pathway

**A private contract membership association**

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — NOT APPROVED — NOT IN EFFECT**

Version 1.0-draft | November 2026

This draft does not replace the source covenant. The source file `membership-covenant` remains the prior text. This version takes effect only if counsel approves it and the Association publishes it and then accepts an application.

This pathway is the membership door on the Temple of Roots website. It emphasizes freedom of association and religious exercise, nature-based spirituality, sanctuary experiences and retreats, community education and training, regenerative living, and spiritual development.

Specific services and activities may require a separate participation agreement, informed consent, a reservation, or other documentation. Signing this Covenant does not reserve a place, does not schedule a ceremony, and does not authorize a health or spiritual practice.

AdBongo LLC is a separate limited liability company. This Covenant does not state, transfer, or change that company's ownership or management. Sanctuary hospitality and ministry activities described here are Association and Temple activities, not a statement that AdBongo LLC owns or manages them.

## 0. Definitions

"Association" means the Advocacy Launchpad Private Membership Association, an unincorporated auxiliary of Temple of Roots, a nature-based ministry that describes its status with reference to IRC § 508(c)(1)(A).

"Temple of Roots" means that nature-based ministry. This Covenant uses the Association's existing description of the Temple. It does not ask a signer to treat that description as a ruling by the Internal Revenue Service.

"AdBongo LLC" means a separate limited liability company. Members may engage it only under a separate written services agreement.

"Blockchain" refers to distributed ledger technologies (DLT), including but not limited to Ricardian smart contracts (v2) that combine legally-enforceable natural language with executable code.

A Ricardian contract, in this Covenant, is one document that a lawyer and a program both read. The lawyer reads this prose. The program reads the same prose, divided at the same section headings, and checks the hashes in Section 16. There is no second contract for the machine.

"Foundational membership" means the voluntary private contractual relationship created when the Association accepts an application. It does not require a purchase, donation, subscription, or NFT.

"Pathway" means the website context through which a person applies. The Temple of Roots pathway and the AdBongo pathway are two doors into the same Association.

"Steward" means a person the Association authorizes to accept or decline applications and to administer membership records.

The applicant signs by electronic signature on the membership page. A wallet signature, a written sigil, or a formal onboarding ceremony may be used as an additional record. None of those is required to sign or to receive foundational membership. Blockchain credentials and Hats Protocol roles are not prerequisites.

## 4. Freedom of Association, Private Contract, Confidentiality, and Jurisdiction

The Association and its members expressly invoke and reserve their rights to freedom of association, peaceful assembly, freedom of contract, privacy, and, where applicable, freedom of religion and religious exercise, as protected by the United States Constitution, the Texas Constitution, and other applicable law.

Membership constitutes a voluntary exercise of the right to associate privately with others for shared lawful purposes, including advocacy, education, spiritual development, technological innovation, and mutual benefit.

Upon execution and acceptance of this Covenant, the applicant enters into a private contractual relationship with the Association. Members voluntarily agree that the Association's internal affairs, governance, membership privileges, confidential communications, and consensual activities shall be governed by this Covenant and the Association's adopted procedures to the fullest extent permitted by law.

The Association reserves the right to establish membership qualifications, standards of conduct, internal governance procedures, and methods of dispute resolution consistent with applicable law.

The Association and its members intend that membership activities be conducted within their private contractual relationship rather than offered indiscriminately to the general public.

Membership does not, by itself, alter citizenship or other legal status or displace mandatory law.

The following source-covenant statements are preserved for legal review and are not deleted:

- All activities within the Association are private and protected under contract law.
- Members agree not to disclose Association matters or fellow members' information to non-members or public authorities.
- This agreement establishes exclusive jurisdiction under private membership law, separate from statutory or maritime jurisdictions.
- The Association is legally independent from Lex.Clinic Hat agreements. Any contracts signed within Lex.Clinic Hat are distinct and do not impose obligations or liabilities on this Association or its members. This agreement and its provisions stand as a separate and autonomous legal framework.

### Member information, records, and access

The Association may keep the member's name, email address, state or province, country, agreement version, electronic signature, submission time, and the record of acceptance or rejection. It keeps those records to administer membership, to give the member a copy, and to maintain an audit trail.

The Association does not publish the member directory, the signature, or the application on a blockchain. It does not place the member's name, email, or signature in a public ledger.

Stewards may see application and membership records as needed to accept, decline, or administer membership. A member may see that member's own record. The signing system stores access tokens only as hashes and does not store wallet private keys.

The Association may disclose a member record when the member asks, when needed to administer this Covenant, when a written legal duty requires it, or when disclosure is reasonably necessary to prevent serious harm. The preserved non-disclosure bullet above is retained on purpose so counsel can reconcile it with mandatory law. It is not silently removed.

Membership records are kept for the life of the membership and for seven years after resignation or termination, and longer while a dispute, audit, or legal duty remains open. Access is limited by role.

## 7. Dispute Resolution and Arbitration

In the event of a dispute between members, or between a member and the Association, the parties agree to resolve the matter through binding arbitration.

Primary arbitration shall be offered in one of the following jurisdictions, as elected by the Board of Stewards:

- Prospera ZEDE, Honduras, under the applicable private arbitration framework recognized by its governing authority; or
- Kleros, a decentralized dispute resolution protocol (https://kleros.io) for peer-reviewed, blockchain-native arbitration.

The parties may mutually agree in writing to the appointment of an independent arbitrator or alternative arbitration forum.

By joining this Association, members expressly waive their right to litigate in public courts and consent to private dispute resolution through one of the above mechanisms. The outcome of such arbitration shall be final and binding unless overridden by a Proposal for Grace approved by the Board of Stewards.

Members further agree to cover their own arbitration costs and recognize that an internal insurance pool or bounty system may be used at the discretion of the Association to support claims, settlements, or equitable resolutions.

For complaints or concerns, contact info@adbongo.io.

Kleros and Prospera are retained as options and are subject to verification before publication. The court-waiver sentence is preserved for legal review and is not deleted.

### Proposed fallback — not adopted

This fallback is proposed for approval. It is not part of the operative clause unless the Association adopts it in a later published version.

If the forum the Board of Stewards elects is unavailable, or if the parties cannot use it, the parties will confer in good faith and may appoint one independent arbitrator in writing. If they do not agree on an arbitrator within 30 days after a written request, either party may ask a court in the State of Texas only to appoint an arbitrator under Chapter 171 of the Texas Civil Practice and Remedies Code. The court appointment request would not submit the merits of the dispute. The seat of that fallback arbitration would be Texas. The Texas General Arbitration Act and, where it applies, the Federal Arbitration Act, would govern the arbitration agreement. Judgment on an award could be entered in a court with jurisdiction only to the extent required to enforce the award.

## 16. Ricardian Form

This Covenant is a Ricardian contract: one document that a lawyer and a program both read. A lawyer reads this prose. A program reads the same prose, divided at these section headings, and checks the hashes in this section. The program does not read a different contract, and it does not add a term that is absent from this prose.

The Ricardian identifier is the SHA-256 hash of the canonical prose. The canonical prose is this Covenant after the filing header, encoded as UTF-8, with every line ending converted to LF, with trailing spaces and tabs removed from each line, with blank lines trimmed from the start and the end, and with one trailing newline. The identifier is computed from that prose. The digest is not written into the prose. A change to the prose produces a different identifier, and a signature on one identifier does not apply to another.

The signing parameters use these keys: acknowledgment, agreementVersion, country, email, fullLegalName, pathway, proseSha256, region, signature, and submittedAt. The region key holds the state or province. Their canonical form is a JSON object with those keys in alphabetical order and no extra whitespace. The parameters hash is the SHA-256 of that JSON. The Ricardian lock is the SHA-256 of the identifier, a newline, and the parameters hash.

A signature record is intact when a program recomputes the identifier from the stored prose and the lock from the stored parameters, and both match the record. That check is the executable part of this contract. It does not require a wallet, a blockchain transaction, or a token.

The member's parameters stay in the membership record. They are not written to a public ledger. The Association may later publish the Ricardian identifier alone. Publishing the identifier does not publish the member's name, email, or signature.

This section identifies the document. It does not change the preserved provisions of this Covenant, and it does not make a draft effective.

**Witness (Member Signature):** the electronic signature captured by the signing system.

**Association acceptance:** a steward's later approval, recorded with the time and the agreement version. Until that acceptance, the application is pending.
