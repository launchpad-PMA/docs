# Optional indemnification alternative

**Status: not adopted. Do not treat this as the covenant.**

The operative indemnification in both version 1.0 drafts is still the source sentence:

> Each member indemnifies and holds harmless the Association, its officers, affiliates, and members from any legal claims, liabilities, or damages arising from participation in the Association. This includes but is not limited to regulatory inquiries, business dealings, and intellectual property matters.

This file is the alternative the Board can approve later. If it is approved, it should replace that sentence in a new version and the redline should show the replacement. It does not replace anything now.

## Proposed clause

**Member indemnity.** The member will indemnify the Association and its stewards against third-party claims, damages, and reasonable defense expenses to the extent they arise out of the member's breach of the Covenant, the member's negligence or willful misconduct, or the member's misuse of membership access, materials, or credentials.

**Defense.** For a covered third-party claim, the Association may choose counsel that is reasonably acceptable to the member. The member pays the reasonable defense expenses of that claim. The Association will give prompt notice, and a failure to give prompt notice relieves the member only to the extent the delay causes prejudice. The member may not settle a claim that imposes an obligation on the Association, or that admits fault by the Association, without the Association's written consent.

**Intellectual property.** The member will indemnify the Association against third-party claims that material the member supplies infringes a copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property right. The Association is responsible for its own materials.

**Regulatory matters.** The member is responsible for penalties that result from the member's own regulated activity. The member is not, under this alternative, required to indemnify the Association for a penalty that results from the Association's own statements, offerings, or operations.

**Exclusions.** This alternative does not require the member to indemnify anyone for that person's fraud, willful misconduct, or gross negligence. It does not apply to a liability that applicable law does not allow to be shifted or waived, including a non-waivable consumer, civil-rights, or statutory remedy. It does not make one member the insurer of another member's dispute with the Association.

**Survival.** This indemnity survives resignation or termination only for claims arising out of events that occurred while the person was a member, and only according to the terms above.

## What this alternative changes

| Topic | Source clause, still operative | This unadopted alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who is protected | Association, officers, affiliates, and members | Association and its stewards |
| Whose conduct | "Arising from participation," which can be read broadly | The member's breach, negligence, willful misconduct, or misuse |
| Kind of claim | Any legal claims, liabilities, or damages | Third-party claims |
| Defense cost | Not addressed | Reasonable defense expenses for a covered third-party claim |
| Intellectual property | Included without a limit | Limited to material the member supplies |
| Regulatory | Included without a limit | Limited to the member's own regulated activity |
| Mandatory law | Not addressed | Non-waivable liabilities stay where the law puts them |
